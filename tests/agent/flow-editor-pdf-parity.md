# Editor and PDF parity

1. Open a saved structured chord chart and select two columns.
2. Confirm editor shows paginated white A4 pages with readable lyric text, balanced final-page columns, and no chord separated from its lyric row.
3. Confirm sections crossing a column or page show a `(continued)` heading.
4. Edit a wrapped lyric segment and drag a chord into another wrapped segment; reload and confirm canonical full lyric and chord placement persist.
5. Export PDF. Confirm no songbook title page exists for one song.
6. Compare every editor page to PDF: identical page count, section columns, lyric wrapping, continuation headings, and full-width bottom credits/arrangement.
7. Confirm PDF lyric type is readable and no page contains only credits or arrangement.
